# **Project Overview**

- Q: What is our task?
    - Predict the current-under-threshold $z < \epsilon$ for a given topography map $X$ and unknown current map $y$.
    - This task bridges the domains of **computer science** and **material informatics**
    - In all honesty, our task is not and most likely *will not* be crystal clear for a little while. Mainly, we are currently exploring what AI applications exist for these highly valuable C-AFM scans of $MoS_2$
- Q: Specifically, *why* do we care about predicting this property $z$?
    1. Grade samples
        - Material uniformity/performance variability
    2. Speed up material analysis/discovery
    3. Sustainable/eco-friendly material design iteration
    - Broadly, the ability to predict the conductivity of a sample of $MoS_2$ from a topography map is highly useful
